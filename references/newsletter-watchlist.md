# Supplementary public newsletter watchlist

These four sources supplement the 53 YouTube creators. They provide research and first-party product context, but they are **not** creator videos and never increase YouTube video counts, independent-creator counts, or cross-channel momentum scores.

| Source | Public archive to scan | Type | Include only |
|---|---|---|---|
| [The Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/email-sign-up/) | [Science & Technology RSS](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/section/science-technology/feed/) and linked Gazette articles | University news | Dated AI research, science, policy, education, or society articles with a substantive finding or expert perspective. The daily digest covers many non-AI topics; filter article by article. |
| [Neon Changelog](https://neon.com/docs/changelog) | [Official changelog](https://neon.com/docs/changelog) | Vendor release notes | Dated changes relevant to AI Gateway, agents, model access, or AI development infrastructure. |
| [Apify](https://apify.com/change-log) | [Official changelog](https://apify.com/change-log) | Vendor release notes | Dated changes relevant to AI agents, web data, MCP, or automation. |
| [Artlist](https://artlist.io/blog/) | [Official blog](https://artlist.io/blog/) | Vendor editorial | Dated, substantive AI video, image, audio, or creator-workflow updates. |

## Collection rules

1. Use the same date window as the YouTube scan. Read each public archive and the original public article or release note; record its title, Chinese translation, publication date, canonical URL, source type, and topic labels. Deduplicate by canonical URL.
2. Include only AI-relevant, substantive items. Skip course enrollment, discounts, webinars, generic promotional messages, unrelated university news, and undated landing pages. Harvard Online course promotions are not part of this watchlist.
3. If a public archive or article is inaccessible, mark that source or item `unavailable`. Do not infer that it had no updates.
4. Treat a vendor's release note as first-party evidence of what that vendor announced, not as an independent evaluation or proof of superiority. Verify consequential research, product, pricing, policy, and technical claims against the linked original source or other primary sources.
5. Keep newsletter findings in a separate report and dataset section. An item may suggest a topic to watch, but it does not establish a multilingual creator trend. Video briefs still require monitored creator-video evidence unless the user explicitly asks for a newsletter-led idea.
6. Collect from public archives. Never save private inbox content, recipient addresses, message IDs, tracking URLs, or personal subscription details in this public repository or its reports.
